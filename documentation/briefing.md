# Briefing

## Mission (What Does It Do Today?)

### Help elderly people in delicate situations, whether homeless or whose children are unable to care for them daily, by sending volunteers to assist them at home or by offering shelter at our center. Ensuring dignity, quality of life, and social inclusion for the elderly population.

## Vision (Where Do You Want to Get To?)

### Transform society so that aging is seen with dignity, autonomy, and respect, actively fighting ageism.

## Values (How Does It Act?)

### Respect for life stories, empathy, transparency, social innovation, equity, affection, and love

---

## Target Audience

### Elderly people, families in vulnerable situations, friends, potential volunteers.

## Website Objective

### Raise donations, recruit volunteers, and provide institutional transparency

---

## Essential Pages

### Home, About Us, Campaigns, Transparency, Volunteer & Sign Up

---

## Donations and Fundraising

### The website will be able to receive online donations and will provide accountability and reports on CDQC's spending

---

## Accessibility

### The website must follow WCAG accessibility guidelines to serve elderly people and those with low vision.

### The website must have contrast in its text to make letters more visible

---

## Visual Identity

### Cool, light colors (blue, green, purple) and warm but not too strong colors (red)

### Clean and inclusive for the elderly population
